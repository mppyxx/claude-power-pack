# Launch brief: Ferrynote 1.0

## What it is
Ferrynote is a shared logbook app for small sailing and rowing clubs. Members sign a boat out and back in from their phone (boat, crew, time out, expected return, conditions, any damage). The club can see who is on the water right now, and the safety officer gets a short daily summary by email.

## Who it is for
Volunteer-run clubs with 10 to 150 members that still use a paper logbook in the boathouse or a WhatsApp group.

## Why we built it
Priya Raman (founder) ran safety at Ashwater Rowing Club for six years. Twice a crew came back late and nobody noticed for over an hour, because the paper log was in the boathouse and the safety officer was at home. Ferrynote started in 2024 as a spreadsheet she built for her own club.

## What is in 1.0
- Sign a boat out or in in under 10 seconds. Works offline and syncs when signal comes back.
- "On the water" board: who is out, in which boat, and when they are due back.
- Overdue alert: if a crew is not back 30 minutes after their expected return, the duty officer gets a text message.
- Damage reports with photos, sent to the boat captain.
- CSV export for insurance and AGM reports.

## Beta numbers
- 14 clubs in the UK and Ireland since May
- 2,300 outings logged
- 11 overdue alerts sent, all resolved safely

## Customer quote

Free for clubs up to 20 members. Larger clubs pay a flat £4 per month, no per-seat fees.
